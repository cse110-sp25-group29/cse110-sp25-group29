# User Stories

## Name: Stuart Student

### Details: High School/University Student looking to expand network both for student connections and outreaching companies/hiring managers. 

### Wants & Needs: 
* Make it quick + easy to add other people to your folder of business cards 
* Easy to access/add information on cards (Ex. professional template that looks appealing and has preset info such as GitHub/LinkedIn and maybe a quick easy way to access resume)
* Notes features to annotate/distinguish new connections (ex. Where they met them)
* Create folders to categorize connections (Ex. Hiring managers, other students, coworkers)
* Some metric to display how “good you are doing” in the realm of networking (Ex. connections, past jobs, etc.)
* Easy to distinguish people’s intention for the app (casual or business)

### Don’ts: 
* Don’t really care about visually appealing UI (compared to a casual user who uses it as decoration)
* Less features to reduce complexity, both on the users and developers side
* “Useless entertaining/engaging features” (Ex. quest/achievement that gives exclusive banner/border, leveling system in terms of cosmetics).

### User Story: 
As a student, I want to prioritize networking by making connections smoothly to people in the industry (other students, coworkers, hiring managers) so that I can get a job when I graduate and learn more about my field of interest.

## Name: Casey Casual

### Details: 
A casual user who uses the app more as a way to express their personality and creativity. Would enjoy seeing others using the same tools to display themselves uniquely.

### Wants & Needs: 
* Lot’s of preset cosmetics/decorations
* Ability to upload your own templates/features
* Easy to access/add information on cards
* Make it quick + easy to add other people to your folder of cards
* Ability to make multiple cards (Ex. could be part of different communities, so displays in depth passion for each
* A space for specific groups/interests
* Easy to distinguish people’s intention for the app (casual or business)

### Don’ts: 
* No ads!
* Identity of the app should not be completely business focused (Ex. neglecting creativity features in favor of business features)

### User Story: 
As a casual user, I want my card to be an expression of myself, featuring a lot of customizability and character, so that I can reflect who I am to other people and learn more about them.

## Name: Preston Professional

### Details:
A professional who has been working for a while. Looking for life-long connections to keep them in the loop/market. Want to keep track of companies they see a future with

### Wants & Needs: 
* Connection filter system (Ex. only adding through QR code to ensure genuine connections)
* Ensures efficiency over aesthetics (Ex. doesn’t want lag or formatting issues)
* In addition to individuals, companies have specific cards that describe their values and work-life balance.
* Actual printable cards that display a QR code that leads to digital card

### Don’ts: 
* Ability to add people spontaneously (Ex. no cold-messaging to add connection)
* Don’t care as much about visual aesthetic (Just wants simple to read professional look)

### User Story: 
As a professional, I want to make selective, meaningful connections in the workforce so that I can maintain a presence in the industry and collaborate with those that truly align with my values/ideals.

## Name: Monica Manager

### Details: 
An executive/hiring manager seeking new hires that align with company values and bring something innovative to the table.

### Wants & Needs: 
* Adding on to the annotation feature, a streamline process to assess other cards (Ex. rating to see their fit in the company)
* Actual printable cards that display a QR code that leads to digital card
* Sectioning of professional business cards (Ex. professional experiences, values, what you have to offer)

### Don’ts: 
* Cold messaging system (undermines professionalism), some sort of permission must be given to connect (Ex. QR Code)

### User Story: 
As an executive/hiring manager, I want to find those who are the best fit for my company so that I can employ those who can bring the most for my company’s growth.
