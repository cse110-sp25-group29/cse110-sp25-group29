## Our Norms & Rules

- Things we always do  
  - Follow the style guide  
  - Create detailed Issues  
  - Acknowledge announcements and messages  
  - For interpersonal conflicts, notify the team leads and develop a resolution. If a satisfactory resolution isn’t reached, bring the issue to the TA.  
- Things we never do  
  - Watch reels at meetings  
  - Accuse or blame someone of XXX / point fingers  
- What do we do when rules are broken?  
  - Assess which rule and how exactly it was broken  
  - Speak one-one with team member to **seek their perspective**  
  - Perform corrective actions to \_\_  
  - Escalate if needed, and reach out to the team lead  
  - If a rule is broken often, review the rule.  
- How do we make decisions?  
  - ALWAYS discuss with the team first  
  - Try to get all stakeholders’ input on it, make sure to reach out to the team leaders.  
  - Reach consensus and record the decision in our shared space  
  - Decision making hierarchy  
    - Sub teams can make decisions that don’t impact other teams  
    - Consult with Team Leaders for bigger Decisions  
    - Consult with TA (if needed) to conform to guidelines.  
- When & How do we Raise Issues/ Ask for help  
  - Daily stand-up: share any blockers or questions  
  - Slack \#general channel: post non-urgent issues or requests  
  - Direct ping: for urgent matters, reach out to the appropriate teammate or lead immediately
